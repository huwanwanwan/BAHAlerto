# Login artwork

Current asset: `../assets/stickers/user-login-icon.png`.
Source: the owner's `user-login-icon.png` from the repository root.
Copied byte-for-byte, preserving its original transparency, colors and pixels.
No image generation or image editing was used for this replacement.

The artwork is a 1536 x 1024 transparent PNG with intentional open space on the
left. The curved header contains the whole image using `BoxFit.contain` and
right alignment. The title and tagline are drawn above it on the left. This
keeps the complete illustration visible at different phone widths, without
cropping the alert symbol, location pin, person or vehicle.

Earlier generated illustrations and prompts remain available in Git history.
