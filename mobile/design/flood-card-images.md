# Flood card illustrations

`assets/flood-street-ph-1.png` and `assets/flood-street-ph-2.png` were generated
for the two Home Nearby Flood Reports cards. They depict illustrative Philippine
barangay street flooding. These are not submitted flood report photographs or
proof of a real flood at the road named on a card. Both images have a visible
AI illustration label in the app. Sources are rendered directly with BoxFit.cover,
without the previous thumbnail/UI cropping workaround. Original source thumbnails
are retained. No database report or verification status was modified.
