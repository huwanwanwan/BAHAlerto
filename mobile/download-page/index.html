What is BAHAlerto?⟶

A project centered on flood awareness and community preparedness. This page introduces its identity and downloadable pictures.

How do I download the pictures?⟶

Select a picture resource above, or download the complete ZIP pack. Your browser will save the file to your device.

Can I download the mobile app?⟶

An installable app release has not been added yet. The phone above is an illustrative preview, not a live flood conditions display.
